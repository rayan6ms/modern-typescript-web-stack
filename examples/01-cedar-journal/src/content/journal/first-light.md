The first thing to notice is the light. It settles between the trees, turning a familiar path into a series of small discoveries: a bright leaf, a dark trunk, a bend disappearing into green.

## Stay with the scene

A photograph begins before the shutter opens. Pause long enough to see where your eye returns. Sometimes the most useful change is a step to the side; sometimes it is simply waiting.

- Look for light before looking for a subject.
- Leave room for the landscape to breathe.
- Make one frame, then look again without the camera.

## A reason to return

The same path will look different tomorrow. Light shifts, leaves turn, and what seemed ordinary becomes the detail that holds a picture together. Keeping a journal is a way of remembering to return.
